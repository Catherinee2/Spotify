# Exploring User Engagement on Music Streaming Platforms with Spotify Data

This project analyzes **user engagement on music streaming platforms** by examining audio features of tracks available on Spotify. The goal is to understand how factors like **danceability, energy, tempo, acousticness**, and more influence user preferences and listening behavior. Insights from this analysis can help improve **personalized recommendations** and **playlist curation strategies**.

##  Project Overview
From the analysis, it is clear that **user engagement with tracks is influenced by a combination of factors rather than any single attribute.**  

Key findings:
- Track features such as **danceability, energy, acousticness, and instrumentalness** show **weak correlations with popularity**, suggesting that listener preferences are **complex and multidimensional**.  
- **Shorter tracks (under 16 minutes)** perform better, while **longer tracks** show a noticeable drop in popularity.  
- **Non-explicit tracks** achieve broader reach and higher popularity, likely due to their accessibility to wider audiences.  
- **Genres** significantly shape user preferences:  
  - Globally popular genres like **Kpop, Anime, and Chill** emphasize cultural and mood-based recommendations.  
  - Relaxing and emotional genres such as **Acoustic and Ambient** highlight demand for **mood-specific playlists**.  
- Attributes like **tempo** and **valence** do not show strong isolated effects, but their impact may emerge in combination with other features.  

## Project Goals
- Analyze the relationship between **track features** and **user engagement**.  
- Identify **patterns in genre preferences** across cultural and emotional contexts.  
- Provide insights to enhance **personalized music recommendations**.  
- Support **streaming platforms** in designing better **playlist curation strategies**.  

##  Dataset
The dataset comes from **[Spotify Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/maharshipandya/-spotify-tracks-dataset)** and contains track-level attributes, including:  
- **Track Info**: ID, Name, Artists, Album  
- **Popularity**: Spotify’s popularity metric  
- **Audio Features**: Danceability, Energy, Acousticness, Instrumentalness, Liveness, Speechiness, Valence, Tempo, Duration  
- **Metadata**: Explicit flag, Time Signature, Genre  


##  Methodology
1. **Data Cleaning & Preparation**: Removed missing/irrelevant values and ensured track features were usable for analysis.  
2. **Exploratory Analysis**: Compared features like danceability, energy, and acousticness against track popularity.  
3. **Genre Insights**: Grouped tracks by genres (global vs. mood-based) to identify cultural and emotional patterns.  
4. **Engagement Patterns**: Examined length of track, explicit vs. non-explicit, and audio features in combination.  
5. **Key Findings**: Summarized insights into what drives engagement.  

## 🔑 Key Insights
- **Engagement is multidimensional** – no single track feature guarantees popularity.  
- **Shorter, non-explicit tracks** are generally more successful.  
- **Genres matter more than isolated audio features**, with cultural and emotional contexts driving user preferences.  
- **Personalized, mood-specific recommendations** are more effective than generic suggestions.
